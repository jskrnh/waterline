Privacy

Last updated 8 September 2026

Waterline is a cash-flow forecasting tool. This page explains what it stores, where that data lives, and how to remove it. It is written to be read, not to be skimmed past.

Waterline never asks for bank logins, card numbers, or account numbers, and has no connection to any financial institution. Everything in your forecast is typed in by you.

If you don't create an account

Nothing leaves your device. Your forecast is held in your browser's local storage on that device only. We cannot see it, and clearing your browser data removes it.

If you create an account

We store two things:

  • Your email address, used to sign you in and nothing else. We do not send marketing email.
  • Your forecast — the labels, amounts, dates and frequencies you enter, plus your starting balance.

Labels are free text, so please avoid typing anything into them that you would not want stored.

Where it is stored

Accounts and forecasts are held with Supabase, our database provider. The site itself is served by Cloudflare. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address as part of that request.

Database rows are protected by row-level security, meaning the database itself refuses to return one account's data to any other account.

What we don't do

  • No advertising, and no data sold or shared with advertisers.
  • No analytics or tracking scripts, and no third-party cookies.
  • No profiling, credit scoring, or automated decisions about you.
  • No sharing with anyone except the providers named above, unless required by law.

How long we keep it

Your forecast is kept until you delete it. Accounts with no sign-in for two years may be removed along with their data.

Deleting your data

Sign in and use Delete account in the top bar. That removes your stored forecast immediately. To also remove the login record itself, email the address below and we will close it.

Under Canadian privacy law (PIPEDA) you may also ask what we hold about you, ask for it to be corrected, or withdraw consent. Email us and we will respond within 30 days.

Children

Waterline is not intended for anyone under 16.

Accuracy, and what this tool is not

Waterline projects forward from figures you supply. It does not know your real bank balance, cannot see pending transactions, and does not account for interest, fees, or overdraft charges. It is a planning aid, not financial advice, and not a substitute for a licensed advisor.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, the date at the top will change and signed-in users will be notified by email.

Contact

Questions, corrections, or deletion requests: privacy@wtrline.com
